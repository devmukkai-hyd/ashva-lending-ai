# Adverse Action and Applicant Communication Standard

# ASHVA CAPITAL INTERNAL CREDIT POLICY MANUAL
## CHAPTER 14: STANDARD GOVERNING DECLINES

### 14.1 Scope and Purpose
This chapter establishes mandatory operational standards, required notifications, permitted reason codes, appeal procedures, and retention schedules for all credit applications processed by Ashva Capital underwriting personnel. Compliance with this chapter is mandatory. Underwriters deviating from these provisions are subject to disciplinary action by the Credit Committee.

### 14.2 Turnaround Time for Declines
14.2.1 Every credit application submitted through the Ashva Capital digital origination portal must reach a final underwriting disposition within seventy-two (72) hours of receiving all mandatory intake documentation.
14.2.2 Where an application is declined, the formal Decline Notice must be dispatched to the applicant via registered electronic mail and SMS within four (4) hours of the adverse determination being logged in the Ashva Capital Core Lending System (ACCLS).
14.2.3 Under no circumstances shall the total elapsed time from initial application submission to dispatch of the Decline Notice exceed ninety-six (96) hours, excluding declared national banking holidays.

### 14.3 Mandatory Content of a Decline Notice
Every Decline Notice generated by an Ashva Capital underwriter must contain the following structural elements in the exact order specified:
1. **Application Reference Number:** The unique alphanumeric identifier generated by ACCLS.
2. **Applicant Entity Details:** Legal business name, registered corporate address, and Principal Borrower PAN or equivalent national tax identifier.
3. **Adverse Determination Statement:** A clear, unambiguous statement that the credit facility has been declined by Ashva Capital.
4. **Primary Decline Reason:** Exactly one (1) reason code selected from the Approved Decline Reason List in Section 14.4, accompanied by the mandatory explanatory text defined therein.
5. **Secondary Decline Reasons (If Applicable):** Up to two (2) additional reason codes from Section 14.4, if multiple underwriting thresholds were breached.
6. **Underwriter Identification:** The employee identification number and corporate title of the reviewing underwriter and the approving Senior Credit Officer.
7. **Appeal Instructions:** A notice detailing the right to appeal, the exact deadline for submission, and the designated electronic mail address (`appeals@ashvacapital.internal`).

### 14.4 Approved Decline Reason List
Underwriters are strictly prohibited from citing any reason, text, or rationale that does not appear in the following exhaustive list. No paraphrasing is permitted.

* **APPROVED_STANDARD:** *Meets every underwriting standard.* (Note: Used exclusively for audit trail logging of fully compliant files where disbursement is blocked by operational constraints).
* **DSCR_BELOW_FLOOR:** *Debt-service coverage ratio is below the sector floor.*
* **THIN_FILE:** *Fewer than 24 months operating, or no active trade line.*
* **ADVERSE_BUREAU:** *Bureau score below 450.*
* **PAST_DELINQUENCY:** *A 90-day delinquency, or 500,000 rupees or more written off.*
* **DOC_MISMATCH:** *Declared revenue differs from verified revenue by more than 10 percent.*
* **SECTOR_CAP:** *The sector has reached its maximum share of the loan book.*
* **LEVERAGE_ABOVE_LIMIT:** *Total debt after the loan exceeds 6.0 times EBITDA.*
* **LIQUIDITY_BELOW_FLOOR:** *Current ratio is below 1.10.*
* **TENOR_ABOVE_LIMIT:** *Requested tenor is longer than the grade or the sector allows.*
* **COLLATERAL_SHORTFALL:** *Collateral is worth less than the cover the grade requires.*
* **FRAUD_HOLD:** *Declared revenue differs from verified revenue by more than 25 percent.*
* **GRADE_REQUIRES_REVIEW:** *Grade C2 or D1: never approved automatically.*
* **INCOMPLETE_APPLICATION:** *A figure needed to test a standard was not supplied.*
* **WITHDRAWN_BY_APPLICANT:** *The applicant withdrew the application.*

### 14.5 Prohibition on Unapproved Reasons
1. Underwriters shall not insert custom text, qualitative judgments, or external data points into the Decline Notice.
2. Citing any reason, code, or rationale not explicitly enumerated in Section 14.4 constitutes a Class 1 policy violation.
3. If an applicant's condition does not map precisely to a single code, the underwriter must select the code representing the primary quantitative breach and, if necessary, list up to two additional codes from Section 14.4.

### 14.6 Appeal Process and Deadline
1. An applicant may submit a formal request for reconsideration (an appeal) of a declined application.
2. **Deadline:** The appeal submission must be received by Ashva Capital within fourteen (14) calendar days of the dispatch date printed on the Decline Notice. Appeals received after 23:59 IST on the fourteenth day shall be rejected automatically without review.
3. **Submission Requirements:** The appeal must include new verifiable financial documentation or proof of remediation regarding the specific reason code cited in the Decline Notice.
4. **Adjudication:** Appeals shall be reviewed by the Ashva Capital Appeals Committee, chaired by the Head of Credit Risk, Marcus Vance. The Appeals Committee shall render a final, non-appealable decision within five (5) business days of receiving the complete appeal package.

### 14.7 Record Retention Period
1. All records pertaining to a declined credit application—including the initial application data, bureau reports, verification worksheets, internal communications, and the transmitted Decline Notice—must be retained in the Ashva Capital secure digital archive.
2. The mandatory retention period is seven (7) years from the exact date and timestamp of the Decline Notice dispatch.
3. Deletion or anonymization of declined application files prior to the expiration of the seven-year retention period is strictly prohibited under the Ashva Capital Data Governance Framework.
