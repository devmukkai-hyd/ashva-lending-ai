# Sector Underwriting Note: IT Services

**ASHVA CAPITAL CREDIT POLICY MANUAL**
**SECTION 14: SECTOR-SPECIFIC UNDERWRITING PROTOCOLS**
**SUB-SECTION 14.4: INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY (IT) SERVICES SME LENDING**

### 1.4.1 Sector Definition and Scope
This sub-section governs credit evaluations for Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) borrowers classified under the IT Services sector, including software development houses, systems integrators, managed service providers, and digital transformation consultancies. All underwriters must adhere strictly to the quantitative limits and qualitative assessments defined herein. Underwriters deviating from these parameters require formal sign-off from Chief Credit Officer Arthur Vance or Credit Committee quorum.

### 1.4.2 Portfolio Concentration Limits
1. Aggregate credit exposure to the IT Services sector shall not exceed **16.0%** of Ashva Capital’s total active loan book.
2. Current live exposure stands at **11.3%** of the loan book as of the last monthly reconciliation (dated October 31, 2023). 
3. Any new loan application that would breach the 16.0% aggregate sector ceiling is auto-declined by the core underwriting system without manual override capability.

### 1.4.3 Working Capital Cycle and Cash Flow Dynamics
1. The typical working capital cycle for approved IT Services SME borrowers is **55 days**, measured from billable hour accrual and milestone submission to final client cash settlement.
2. Underwriters must adjust cash flow projections to account for delayed enterprise payment terms, which frequently stretch to net-60 or net-90 days for Tier-1 corporate clients.
3. Seasonal Peak Adjustment: The IT Services sector exhibits **no marked season**. Revenue streams derived from recurring SaaS contracts, retained maintenance agreements, and multi-year staff augmentation contracts provide intra-year stability. Consequently, underwriters shall not apply seasonal revenue uplift factors or cyclical working capital buffers.

### 1.4.4 Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) Floor
1. The baseline DSCR floor for IT Services SME lending is **1.25x**, aligning with Ashva Capital’s institutional standard. 
2. No downward discretion or synthetic DSCR adjustment is permitted for asset-light business models lacking hard immovable property.

### 1.4.5 Primary Causes of Financial Distress
Underwriters must explicitly evaluate and document mitigants against the two primary historical causes of distress in this sector:
1. *Key Person and Talent Attrition Dependency:* Loss of principal software architects, founders, or key client-facing engineers leading directly to contract termination and revenue contraction. Mitigation requires mandatory key-person life insurance assignments held by Ashva Capital.
2. *Client Concentration Risk:* Over-reliance on a single enterprise client for more than **30.0%** of top-line revenue. Mitigation requires structural covenants mandating immediate notification if any single client contract contributes in excess of this threshold.

### 1.4.6 Loan Structuring and Tenor Limits
1. The maximum permissible loan tenor for IT Services SME facilities is **48 months**.
2. Facilities exceeding 36 months require dual-signoff from the Senior Underwriting Manager and Head of Portfolio Risk.

### 1.4.7 Acceptable Collateral Types
Due to the intangible nature of IT service assets, acceptable collateral is strictly restricted to the following instruments, valued in accordance with Ashva Capital Collateral Valuation Schedules:
1. First-ranking general floating charge over all present and future book debts, trade receivables, and contract receivables, maintained at a minimum collateral cover ratio of **135.0%** of the outstanding facility balance.
2. Corporate guarantees from operating parent entities or joint and several personal guarantees from all equity holders holding greater than **20.0%** ordinary share capital.
3. Cash collateral or debt service reserve accounts (DSRA) held on deposit with Ashva Capital equivalent to a minimum of **3 months** of principal and interest servicing obligations.
