# Ashva Capital Credit Policy Manual: Core Underwriting Standards

**ASHVA CAPITAL DIGITAL LENDING DIVISION**  
**CREDIT POLICY MANUAL: SME TERM LOANS**  
**DOCUMENT REF: CPM-SME-2024-V4**  
**EFFECTIVE DATE: 01 JANUARY 2024**

---

### SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

**1.1** This manual establishes the binding underwriting standards for all SME term loans originated by Ashva Capital. Underwriters, Credit Officers, the Credit Committee, and the Board Credit Committee are strictly bound by the thresholds, definitions, and approval hierarchies set forth herein.

**1.2** The automated underwriting engine and manual underwriters shall apply these rules without deviation. No exceptions to numerical thresholds may be granted except through the formal exception process managed by Chief Risk Officer Marcus Vance.

---

### SECTION 2: ELIGIBILITY AND OPERATING HISTORY

**2.1** Every applicant business must demonstrate a minimum operating history of 24 months from the date of incorporation or formal business registration to the date of application. 

**2.2** Any application associated with a business operating for less than 24 months shall be declined automatically by the system.

---

### SECTION 3: CREDIT GRADING AND BUREAU SCORE THRESHOLDS

**3.1** The credit grading of an applicant is determined by its commercial bureau score. The minimum bureau score required for each credit grade is defined as follows:
*   Grade A1: Minimum score of 750
*   Grade A2: Minimum score of 700
*   Grade B1: Minimum score of 650
*   Grade B2: Minimum score of 600
*   Grade C1: Minimum score of 550
*   Grade C2: Minimum score of 500
*   Grade D1: Minimum score of 450
*   Grade D2: Any score below 450

**3.2** A bureau score below 450 classifies the record as an adverse bureau record.

**3.3** A past delinquency is defined strictly as either a 90-day delinquency on any credit facility, or a written-off amount of 500,000 rupees or more in the applicant’s credit history.

---

### SECTION 4: FINANCIAL COVENANTS AND METRICS

**4.1** **Debt-Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR):** 
*   **Definition:** DSCR is defined as EBITDA divided by total annual debt service. Total annual debt service equals the existing annual debt service plus the first twelve monthly instalments on the proposed loan. 
*   **Calculation Parameters:** The first twelve monthly instalments on the proposed loan shall be calculated on a reducing balance at the assessment rate of 14.00 percent per annum.
*   **Minimum Threshold:** The minimum required DSCR is 1.25x, or the applicable sector floor where that sector floor is higher than 1.25x.

**4.2** **Leverage Limit:** 
*   Maximum leverage is capped at 6.0 times EBITDA. Total debt—comprising existing debt plus the proposed loan—may not exceed 6.0x EBITDA.

**4.3** **Liquidity Floor:** 
*   The current-ratio floor is set at 1.10. Any applicant with a current ratio below 1.10 fails this standard.

---

### SECTION 5: WORKED DSCR CALCULATION EXAMPLE

**5.1** Underwriters shall use the following standard formula and methodology for all DSCR evaluations:
*   EBITDA: 2,400,000 rupees
*   Existing Annual Debt Service: 600,000 rupees
*   Proposed Loan: 2,000,000 rupees over a 48-month tenor
*   First twelve monthly instalments at the assessment rate of 14.00 percent: 655,835 rupees
*   Total Annual Debt Service: 600,000 + 655,835 = 1,255,835 rupees
*   **DSCR Calculation:** $2,400,000 / 1,255,835 = 1.91x$

---

### SECTION 6: DECISION PATHWAY RULES

**6.1** **Decline Criteria:** An application must be declined immediately if any of the following conditions are met:
1.  DSCR is below 1.00x.
2.  The bureau record is adverse (bureau score below 450).
3.  The business has operated for less than 24 months.
4.  The assigned credit grade is D2.

**6.2** **Borderline Criteria:** An application is classified as borderline and must be referred to a human reviewer if any of the following conditions are met:
1.  Any financial or operational standard (other than an automatic decline trigger) is missed.
2.  There is a past delinquency on record.
3.  The requested tenor is longer than allowed by the assigned credit grade or sector policy.
4.  The collateral value is worth less than the cover required by the credit grade.
5.  A figure or document needed to test a standard is missing from the file.
6.  The assigned credit grade is C2 or D1.

**6.3** **Approvable Criteria:** If an application does not trigger a decline under Clause 6.1 and does not trigger a borderline review under Clause 6.2, it is classified as approvable.

---

### SECTION 7: AUTOMATED VS. HUMAN APPROVAL AUTHORITY

**7.1** **Automated Approval:** The underwriting system may approve an application automatically *only* when all of the following three conditions are simultaneously met:
1.  The application is classified as approvable under Clause 6.3.
2.  The requested loan amount is no more than 1,000,000 rupees.
3.  The credit grade is C1 or better (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1).

**7.2** **Mandatory Human Review:** Any application failing even one of the automated approval criteria in Clause 7.1—including any application requiring a decline—requires human sign-off.

---

### SECTION 8: LOAN-SIZE TIERS AND APPROVAL AUTHORITIES

**8.1** Approval authority is strictly segregated by loan size and governance tiers:
*   **Tier 1 (Up to 1,000,000 rupees):** Authorized Credit Officer.
*   **Tier 2 (Up to 5,000,000 rupees):** Credit Committee (minimum quorum of two designated Credit Committee members).
*   **Tier 3 (Any size larger than 5,000,000 rupees):** Board Credit Committee.

**8.2** **Named Signatory Mandate:** No loan above 5,000,000 rupees may be approved without a named human signatory from the Board Credit Committee, specifically Chief Credit Officer Sarah Jenkins or Board Credit Chair Arthur Pendelton.

---

### SECTION 9: PRICING BASE RATE

**9.1** The Ashva Capital lending base rate is established at 9.50 percent per annum. All risk-based spreads must be added to this base rate in accordance with the credit grade pricing matrix maintained by the Treasury Department.
